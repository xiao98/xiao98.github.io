Research

My main interests are reinforcement learning and autonomous AI systems. I am interested in how learning, memory, and reliable evaluation can support agents that work over longer time horizons.

Research interests

Reinforcement learning for LLM reasoning and agents

I am interested in reinforcement learning methods for improving language-model reasoning, planning, and decision-making, including online learning, exploration, and verifiable rewards. A question I would like to study is how feedback from multi-step interactions can produce strategies that generalize across tasks.

Self-improving and long-horizon agents

I am interested in persistent memory, experience reuse, self-generated training data, and recursive self-improvement (RSI). How can an agent improve its own problem-solving process while distinguishing real progress from unreliable self-evaluation?

Verification and scientific agents

Programming, mathematics, and scientific simulation provide environments where actions can be checked through tests, formal constraints, or numerical solvers. I would like to explore how these forms of feedback can support more reliable autonomous learning and reasoning.
