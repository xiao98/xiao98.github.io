Interests

Art and creative practice

Before focusing on mathematics and artificial intelligence, I worked as a film director and photographer. Moving images and visual storytelling remain an important part of my creative practice.

My interests extend from filmmaking and photography to experimental video, web-based art, and computational visual expression. I am interested in how digital interfaces and algorithms can become materials for artistic work.

Philosophy

My reading in philosophy centres on Nietzsche, on Spinoza (largely through Deleuze), on Kojève's reading of Hegel, and on Foucault's late lectures. I also read in ancient and medieval philosophy, psychoanalysis, and the philosophy of technology, from Norbert Wiener's cybernetics to Yuk Hui.

Quantitative trading

I am interested in quantitative trading and cryptocurrency markets, particularly time-series analysis, out-of-sample evaluation, and the difficulty of separating useful signals from noise and overfitting.

Quant ML Pipeline

An experimental financial machine-learning pipeline focused on walk-forward evaluation, data-leakage checks, transaction costs, and baseline comparisons. The emphasis is on testing whether a signal survives realistic evaluation, rather than assuming a profitable strategy.
