Interests

Art and creative practice

Before focusing on mathematics and artificial intelligence, I worked as a film director and photographer. Moving images and visual storytelling remain an important part of my creative practice.

My interests extend from filmmaking and photography to experimental video, web-based art, and computational visual expression. I am interested in how digital interfaces and algorithms can become materials for artistic work.

Live neural network · Web

A neural network trained live in the browser to reproduce whatever is drawn on the screen, shown as a three-dimensional object: every line is a weight, every point a neuron or a pixel. What the network drew before stays behind it in layers, so that time becomes depth.

Philosophy

Quantitative trading

I am interested in quantitative trading and cryptocurrency markets, particularly time-series analysis, out-of-sample evaluation, and the difficulty of separating useful signals from noise and overfitting.

Most of this work is pre-registered falsification: the hypothesis, the parameter grid, and the pass criteria are frozen before any backtest, and a script issues the verdict. Almost every hypothesis so far has failed, and the results are listed as they came out.

Evaluation tools

  • Backtest statistics A small library used by every project below: Probabilistic and Deflated Sharpe Ratio (Bailey and López de Prado), probability of backtest overfitting by combinatorially symmetric cross-validation, and position sizing that combines a significance gate, a liquidation-safe leverage cap, and fractional Kelly.
  • Quant ML Pipeline An experimental financial machine-learning pipeline built around walk-forward evaluation, data-leakage checks, transaction costs, and baseline comparisons. GitHub

Replicating published strategies

  • Strategy Harvest 100 110 published strategies (WorldQuant 101 alphas, academic factors, CTA and risk-premia rules, crypto-native signals) backtested on crypto perpetuals, equity-index futures, and commodity futures. Of 298 strategy and asset-class cells, none passes the joint gate on deflated Sharpe, PSR, false discovery rate, and ruin probability.
  • Strategy Zoo 75 published strategies on Binance perpetuals across timeframes, with leakage tests and bit-identical reruns. 0 of 229 cells pass.
  • US Equity Zoo The same machine for ETF strategies, judged on alpha against buy-and-hold SPY rather than raw return. 0 of 80 cells pass; twelve-month absolute momentum on SPY, for example, has a raw Sharpe of 1.05 and an alpha Sharpe of −0.57.
  • WorldQuant 101 on crypto Three rounds on a basket of perpetuals. One alpha stays positive out of sample at a Sharpe near 0.3, which is too weak to trade alone.

Crypto: direction and volatility

  • BTC/ETH edge hunt 708 pre-registered trials in seven strategy families on 1h, 4h, and 1d perpetuals, with seven walk-forward folds and a sealed 90-day lockbox. None passes; the best median one-year multiple with ruin probability under 2% is 1.40×. An intraday version reaches 1.51×.
  • Rough volatility for ETH A rough fractional stochastic volatility forecast of daily realized variance against the HAR-RV baseline over 2,364 days. ETH volatility is rough (Hurst exponent near 0.1), but the forecast does not beat HAR (QLIKE 0.422 against 0.385).
  • Mechanised Wyckoff spring A discretionary four-step Wyckoff setup turned into fixed rules with order-flow confirmation. Pooled over a 20-coin basket, the out-of-sample Sharpe is +0.026 on 43 trades.
  • 18-cell discretionary framework A position, trend, and direction table with a 2:1 target, mechanised on the top-20 perpetuals at 4h: 4,625 trades over 4.4 years, at −0.12R per trade after costs.

Cross-sectional factors and risk premia

  • Meme-coin factors Seven cross-sectional factors on 61 meme-coin perpetuals with a survivorship-free universe and a three-day rebalance. 0 of 42 cells pass; the in-sample Sharpe changes sign out of sample.
  • A-share small-cap factors The same method on its home market: 5,533 stocks including 326 delisted, with limit-up and limit-down execution constraints. The combined long-short factor has a Sharpe of 1.90 with no out-of-sample decay (1.55 in sample, 2.42 out), but fails the pre-registered deflated-Sharpe gate.
  • Capacity niches and risk premia Three hypotheses on perpetuals, with slippage taken from real order-book depth over 499 point-in-time symbols: long-tail funding harvest (net Sharpe −1.26), the first 72 hours of new listings (−3.92), and multi-asset volatility-targeted trend following (0.28, not significant).

Forward tests and market microstructure

  • Crypto Momentum Lab Two paper strategies on small-cap perpetuals, both long-only breakouts at funding-rate extremes, tested forward rather than in backtest.
  • Squeeze Forward The same idea moved to US small caps: short interest above 20% of float, at least three days to cover, and a breakout on volume. Forward paper trading only, to avoid survivorship bias.
  • Listing-ladder event study Buying on a smaller exchange five minutes after a Binance listing announcement and holding for 24 hours. Ten events against a pre-registered minimum of 25, so the verdict is insufficient data.
  • Long-tail market making A pre-registered maker experiment on long-tail Binance perpetuals, staged from shadow fills to testnet, and judged from append-only fill logs.
  • Discretionary scorecard A forward scorecard for discretionary trades: each trade is logged and committed before entry, and a script issues the verdict.
